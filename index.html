Welcome

This showcase brings together student teams who have designed and built projects during the year. You can meet the people behind the work, ask questions, and see ideas in action.

What is on show

There are three main areas to explore. One area focuses on creative uses of code. Another looks at tools that help with studying and inclusion. A third takes technology outside with small robots and sensors.

Where to go next

Visit the Exhibits page to read a short description for each area and see an image for each one. If you are planning your trip, open the Visit page for practical details.
