Essentials

Date: Saturday 15 March 2025

Place: MacRobert Building, University of Aberdeen

Time: 10:00 to 16:00

Travel

On foot or cycle

It is a short walk from King Street. Cycle stands are at the main entrance.

Public transport

Buses stop on King Street and Bedford Road. Please check times before you set off.

By car

On site parking is limited. Please use public transport if you can.

Access

There is step free entry to the exhibit areas and accessible toilets on the ground floor. Signs are clear and walkways are wide. A quiet corner is available for visitors who prefer a low sensory space.
