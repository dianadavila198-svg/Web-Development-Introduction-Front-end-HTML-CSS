# `exhibits.html` — EXHIBITS

<h1>Exhibits</h1>

<!--Start of meta description-->
Meta Description: Read a short overview of each exhibit area and see one image for each. Use this page to decide what you want to see first.
<!--End of meta description-->

<!--Start of overview-->
<h2>Overview</h2>
The showcase is arranged so you can move around easily. Each area below has a short paragraph and a single image. The captions help you understand what you are looking at.
<!--End of overview-->

<!--Start of area 1-->
<h2 id="creative-computing">Creative Computing</h2>
<!-- Image file: images/image_creative.png -->
<!-- Alt text: Abstract collage of colourful shapes representing creative coding -->
<!-- Caption: Creative coding and interactive media -->
This area explores sound, pictures, and simple interaction made with code. You may see small visual pieces, tiny games, and artworks that change over time.
<!--End of area 1-->

<!--Start of area 2-->
<h2 id="learning-technologies">Learning Technologies</h2>
<!-- Image file: images/image_educational.png -->
<!-- Alt text: Stylised icon set of books, laptop, and accessibility symbol -->
<!-- Caption: Tools that support teaching and learning -->
Here you will find ideas that help people study and take part. Some projects make reading and note taking easier. Others help teachers run activities in class.
<!--End of area 2-->

<!--Start of area 3-->
<h2 id="outdoor-robotics-iot">Outdoor Robotics and IoT</h2>
<!-- Image file: images/image_outdoor.png -->
<!-- Alt text: Illustration of a small rover beside a sensor tower in a park -->
<!-- Caption: Robots and sensors for outdoor environments -->
This area takes technology outside. There are small rovers, simple sensors, and low power links that gather information and act on it in open spaces.
<!--End of area 3-->

<!--Start of choosing what to see-->
<h2>Choosing what to see</h2>
If you have limited time, start with the area that matches your interests, then return for the others. Staff and students are happy to point you in the right direction.
<!--End of choosing what to see-->

<p><strong>Place:</strong> MacRobert Building, University of Aberdeen</p>
<p><strong>Time:</strong> 10:00 to 16:00</p>
<!--End of essentials-->

<!--Start of travel-->
<h2>Travel</h2>

<h3>On foot or cycle</h3>
It is a short walk from King Street. Cycle stands are at the main entrance.

<h3>Public transport</h3>
Buses stop on King Street and Bedford Road. Please check times before you set off.

<h3>By car</h3>
On site parking is limited. Please use public transport if you can.
<!--End of travel-->

<!--Start of access-->
<h2>Access</h2>
There is step free entry to the exhibit areas and accessible toilets on the ground floor. Signs are clear and walkways are wide. A quiet corner is available for visitors who prefer a low sensory space.
<!--End of access-->
