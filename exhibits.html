Overview

The showcase is arranged so you can move around easily. Each area below has a short paragraph and a single image. The captions help you understand what you are looking at.

Creative Computing

This area explores sound, pictures, and simple interaction made with code. You may see small visual pieces, tiny games, and artworks that change over time.

Learning Technologies

Here you will find ideas that help people study and take part. Some projects make reading and note taking easier. Others help teachers run activities in class.

Outdoor Robotics and IoT

This area takes technology outside. There are small rovers, simple sensors, and low power links that gather information and act on it in open spaces.

Choosing what to see

If you have limited time, start with the area that matches your interests, then return for the others. Staff and students are happy to point you in the right direction.
